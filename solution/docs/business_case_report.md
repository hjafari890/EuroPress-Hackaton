## 1. Executive Summary: Two Customer Problems Solved at the Source

Field interviews and our forensic audit of the `18,477` completed lifts in the competition fleet reveal two systemic blind spots that cost waste generators hundreds of thousands of euros per year:

### Problem 1: Customers Call for Pickup Too Early ("Am I Actually Full?")
Today, a site manager or automated fill-level trigger only sees **platen stroke / ultrasonic volume % (`device_fill_pct_before`)**. Because volume does not measure compacted mass:
- **Median volume fill at pickup is `72.2%`, but median true weight fill is only `64.6%`.**
- **`199` lifts (`â‚¬36,222` avoidable haulage cost)** were triggered when bulky cardboard or dry film bridged across the hopper throat (**Scenario `S40`**), causing the volume sensor to report `88â€“97% FULL` while the container held `<38%` of its target mass capacity.
- **`523` lifts (`â‚¬79,676` avoidable haulage cost)** were triggered by Friday afternoon or pre-holiday precautionary customer calls (**Scenario `S39`**) on `ON_CALL` compactors out of fear that the container might overflow before the hauler's `18â€“30 hour` order-to-lift lead time elapsed.
- **`1,631` lifts (`â‚¬237,689` avoidable haulage cost)** occurred on rigid fixed-schedule routes (**Scenario `S01`**) at `<40%` fill where hygiene rules permitted lower frequency or on-call switching.

**Our Solution â€” The "Empty Only When Truly Full" Max-Payload Optimizer:**
By fusing our source mass measurement ($m_{\text{source}}$) with platen volume ($V_{\text{fill}}$), SMART computes **Effective Bulk Density ($\rho_{\text{eff}} = m_{\text{source}} / V_{\text{fill}}$)** and compares real-time mass accumulation against each hauler's regional **Order-to-Lift Lead-Time Distribution ($L_{\text{op},90}$)**:
1. **Bridging Filter (`S40`):** When $V_{\text{fill}} \ge 82\%$ but $\rho_{\text{eff}} < 0.42\,\rho_{\text{nominal}}$, SMART suppresses the false-full pickup call and triggers an automated high-force anti-bridging crush cycle.
2. **Weekend & Lead-Time Guard (`S39`):** Gives the site manager a live green/amber countdown (*"Safe through Sunday 22:00 â€” order Tuesday 08:30 so truck arrives at 96% target payload"*), eliminating panic calls while preventing overflow.

### Problem 2: The 30â€“45 Day Waste Operator Weight Blackout
Waste operators control the certified weighbridge and the monthly invoice:
- **`4,908` completed lifts (`26.6%` of the fleet, led by `OP-D`) have zero weighbridge tickets ever shared** with the customer.
- **`2,991` completed lifts have weighbridge tickets delayed > 7 days** (median `22 days` for `OP-C`, reaching up to **`116.8 days`**).
- Meanwhile, **`36` of `52` customers (`69%`)** have at least one recyclable contract (`cardboard`, `plastic_film`, or `mixed_plastic`) where `credit_share_bp = 0`â€”meaning the customer receives `â‚¬0/t` credit on valuable secondary raw materials.

**Our Solution â€” Day-0 Source-Weight Receipt & Automated Invoice Audit:**
Equipping every Europress `COMBI`, `DUO`, and `Baler` with source weighing (`Tier 1: SMART-Weight Lite` at `â‚¬74` BOM or `Tier 2: SMART-Weight Verify` at `â‚¬192` BOM) generates an **Instant Day-0 Source-Weight Receipt** the second the truck sets the container down, enabling same-day ERP accrual and automated cent-by-cent verification when the operator's monthly invoice arrives 30 days later.

## 6. Assumptions, Uncertainties & What Remains Untested (`5%` Criterion)

We state our engineering and commercial boundaries transparently:
1. **Unproven Customer Willingness-to-Pay (WTP):** While the synthetic fleet shows a median realisable saving of `â‚¬12,266/yr` for mid-market customers (`6â€“15` devices), real-world WTP and procurement adoption rates (`15% / 25% / 40%`) have not been validated through live customer sales trials.
2. **Physical Prototype & Winter Environmental Validation Pending:** Our `Tier 2` shear-pin + elastomer load-pad architecture (`Â±2.6%` at 95% confidence) is grounded in rigid-body statics and `10,342` simulated weighbridge comparisons, but physical validation on a live `EPC-20 COMBI` and `EPD-24 DUO` at Europress's Kerava factory (including `-25 Â°C` ice build-up under DUO rear wheels and clamp hysteresis) remains to be executed.
3. **Operator Commercial Response:** In real waste markets, operators may contest non-OIML-certified source weights during formal invoice disputes. Our design mitigates this by using source weight as an **audit trigger** to demand the operator's certified weighbridge ticket (`REQUEST_WEIGHBRIDGE_DATA`) and by flagging only statistically sustained discrepancies (`>10%` and `>100 kg`).
4. **Operational & Hygiene Constraints:** As demonstrated in `Task C`, reducing pickup frequency solely on average fill can trigger summer biowaste odour violations or peak-campaign overflows. Our engine enforces hard safety guardrails (`full_hours_28d <= 4`, `P(permissible) >= 0.85`) and requires **human sign-off** before any schedule or threshold change takes effect.

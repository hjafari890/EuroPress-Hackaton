## Challenge Summary

The hackathon is divided into three core deliverables:

1. **Deliverable 1: Weighing Concept**
   - Design accurate, reliable, cost-effective payload weight measurement techniques for Europress industrial waste compactors (e.g. CombiMax, DuoMax).
   - Provide executive summaries, bill of materials (BOM), installation protocols, and sensor trade-off analyses.
   - *Location in repo:* [`03_Deliverable_1_Weighing_Concept/`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/03_Deliverable_1_Weighing_Concept/)

2. **Deliverable 2: SMART Material Value Engine (AI & Analytics)**
   - **Task A (Fill-Level & Pickup Timing):** Predict true compactor fill percentage and forecast optimal pickup dates from hourly hydraulic telemetry.
   - **Task B (Invoice Anomaly Detection):** Identify billing anomalies, uncontracted fee overages, and disputed line items with rule + ML hybrid logic.
   - **Task B2 (Operator Scorecard):** Score operator contamination rates, off-hours access, and compliance.
   - **Task C (Next-Best-Action Optimization):** Recommend optimal economic collection actions (empty now, postpone, re-route).
   - **Task C2 (Operator Action Plans):** Prescribe targeted operator coaching, fine assessments, and equipment maintenance.
   - *Location in repo:* [`solution/engine/`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/solution/engine/) & [`solution/predictions/`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/solution/predictions/)

3. **Deliverable 3: Interactive Web Cockpit & Executive Decision Support**
   - Interactive web-based dashboard allowing operators and executives to inspect fleet status, verify AI decisions, run what-if ROI simulations, and export audit trails.
   - *Location in repo:* [`solution/frontend/`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/solution/frontend/) & [`solution/backend/`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/solution/backend/)
   - *One-Click Launchers:* [`run_cockpit.bat`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/run_cockpit.bat), [`run_cockpit.py`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/run_cockpit.py), [`run_demo.ps1`](file:///d:/Academic%20Resources/Oulu/period%205/EuroPress-Hackaton/run_demo.ps1)
